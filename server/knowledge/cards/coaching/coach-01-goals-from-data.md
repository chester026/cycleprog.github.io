## Summary
A useful goal sits between what the rider wants and what their data say is reachable in the time available. The coach's task is to connect the two: establish the current baseline, estimate a realistic rate of change, translate the wish into measurable targets and into weekly actions, and state honestly where the gap is large. Goals come from the rider; the numbers and the route come from the data.

## Method
1. **Wish**: ask what the rider wants and by when, in their words (an event, a climb, keeping up with a group, riding more, feeling fitter).
2. **Baseline**: recent weekly hours and consistency (last 6–8 weeks), longest ride, power at key durations or speed/heart-rate equivalents, strengths along the power curve.
3. **Demands**: what the goal requires: duration, climbing, likely W/kg or power, skills, fuelling.
4. **Gap and rate**: compare; apply realistic rates. Approximate annual gains in threshold power: first-year riders 10–25%; riders with 1–3 years of training 5–10%; long-trained riders 1–4%. Long-ride duration can extend by about 15–30 min per week.
5. **Targets**: one outcome or event goal, two or three performance markers with dates, and process goals for the week (see `psy-05-goal-types`).
6. **Check feasibility** against available hours and life; adjust the goal or the timeline, not the laws of adaptation.
7. **Review** every 4–8 weeks against data.

## In practice
- When data are thin (no power meter, few rides), set process goals first and let performance targets follow after 4–6 weeks.
- Offer a main goal and a fallback.
- Make at least one early marker easy to reach, to bank a success.
- Avoid goals based on body weight; express climbing goals as power and pacing.
- A goal of "ride regularly and enjoy it" is complete and valid.

## Limits
Rates of improvement vary widely between individuals; forecasts are ranges.

## Coach guidance
Be honest about what is realistic without deflating: show the path, the first step and what would have to be true. Never set a goal the rider did not ask for.
