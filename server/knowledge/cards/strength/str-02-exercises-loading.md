## Summary
A cyclist's strength programme is short and simple: a few multi-joint leg exercises through a cycling-like range, done heavy, with full recovery between sets, plus a small amount of trunk and upper-body work. Technique and gradual loading come before weight. The intention to move the load fast on the way up, even when it moves slowly, is part of the stimulus.

## Key numbers
- Core lower-body exercises (choose 3–4): squat or half squat, leg press (two-leg or single-leg), deadlift or Romanian deadlift, step-up or split squat, hip thrust, standing hip flexion, calf raise.
- Introduction (2–4 weeks): 2–3 sets of 10–15 reps, light to moderate, learning technique; expect soreness.
- Build (8–12 weeks): 3 sets of 4–10 reps at loads that leave 1–2 reps in reserve (about 80–90% of 1RM for the lower rep range); rest 2–3 min between sets.
- Tempo: controlled lowering (2–3 s), fast intent on the lift.
- Range: knee angle from about 90° to near extension matches the pedal stroke; deeper is fine if technique allows.
- Session length: 30–45 min; 2 sessions per week, at least 48 h apart.
- Progression: add load when the top of the rep range is reached in all sets (typically 2.5–5% steps).
- Supplementary: 2–3 trunk exercises, 1–2 upper-body pulls/pushes, 2 sets each.

## In practice
- Single-leg work addresses side differences and needs less absolute load.
- No gym: heavy single-leg bodyweight variations, weighted step-ups, kettlebell or backpack loading can cover the introduction; true heavy loading eventually needs equipment.
- Warm up with 5–10 min easy and 1–2 lighter sets.
- Stop a set when technique breaks down; training to failure is unnecessary.
- Back pain, knee pain or dizziness during lifting: stop and get technique or health checked.

## Limits
Exact exercise choice matters less than load and consistency. Guidance on technique cannot be given safely by text alone.

## Coach guidance
Give the structure (exercises, sets, reps, effort) and point beginners to in-person instruction for technique. Do not prescribe maximal testing to novices.
